Consulting & Cloud Migration

A clear plan for a successful cloud journey

With more and more organisations looking to the cloud to modernise mission-critical applications, we are committed to making your Cloud Migration both seamless and successful.

Cloud Migration

A partner who knows the journey back to front

Whether your plans involve migrating ERP, CRM or other critical applications from on-premise to the cloud, you need a clear plan — and ideally a partner who understands every step of the journey.

We work with our customers to help them achieve their cloud migration tasks. This is especially relevant if you are also considering Process Mining and RPA solutions, as most of these are located in the cloud.

Getting your checklist right

The do’s and don’ts of migration

We’ll help you understand what matters and plan for it. The list is almost endless — but every item is critical to success.

  • Start planning and set a course — and a budget
  • Understand what the benefits are
  • Exercise caution and minimise disruption
  • Agree the migration-architect role
  • Choose the level of cloud integration you require
  • Decide between single cloud and multi-cloud
  • Establish cloud KPIs
  • Prioritise tasks
  • Communicate transparently with your colleagues
  • Create a data-migration plan
  • Review resource allocation

70%

According to recent research, as many as 70% of all Digital Transformation projects either fail or stall.

Digital Transformation and Business Process Management initiatives are far from easy.

Why projects fail

There are usually a number of familiar reasons. The project:

  • was poorly conceived or poorly defined
  • did not have full C-level commitment
  • did not include the necessary due diligence up front
  • was more complex than appreciated
  • was under-invested
  • did not include clear objectives or goals
