Process Mining first

Five simple steps to automation that pays off

Jumping into RPA without first understanding your processes can be expensive. Our approach puts insight before automation.

  1. Discover

    Visualise and analyse your current processes from real event-log data.

  2. Improve

    Spot bottlenecks and inefficiencies — then improve or remove them.

  3. Implement

    Streamline processes and identify the best candidates for RPA.

  4. Automate

    Use AI to find the best path, then automate the most profitable processes.

  5. Monitor

    Continuously measure automation rates, speed and cost savings.
