Our Unique Approach

Process Mining first — by design, not by chance

Our decision to specialise in both Process Mining and Robotic Process Automation was deliberate.

RPA is rightly considered the answer to many challenges, such as getting high-volume tasks done quickly and without errors. But it would be wholly wrong to implement RPA without first knowing exactly how your business processes work.

How can you improve process efficiency with robotics without first understanding the current state of your processes and day-to-day operations? How can you improve what you don’t know?

That’s why we put Process Mining first.

What Process Mining gives you

  • Insight into how your processes actually run
  • Process bottlenecks, compliance violations and the root causes of problems
  • The processes you should automate first
  • An overview of your automation progress
  • Proof of the value — in time and money — that RPA will deliver

Why RPA alone can be costly

RPA can be very expensive if implemented incorrectly. Common pitfalls include:

  • Picking the wrong processes to automate
  • Focusing on attended desktop automation instead of unattended automation
  • Not being sure what outcomes to expect
Five Simple Steps

From discovery to continuous improvement

There are many reasons why jumping in with an RPA solution before conducting Process Mining can be an expensive move. Instead, there are five simple steps to consider:

  1. Discover

    Visualise and analyse your current processes based on real-time information — including time-stamped event-log data — to know what’s happening in your daily operations.

  2. Improve

    With a live picture of your processes, it’s easy to spot bottlenecks or inefficiencies and improve or remove them.

  3. Implement

    Use process insight to streamline — remove or automate — processes where needed, and see the most suitable candidates for RPA deployment.

  4. Automate

    Employ AI to find the best path for your processes, which you can then optimise. Automate the most profitable processes.

  5. Monitor

    Use Process Mining to monitor processes and automation rates continuously. Measure and report speed, cost and time savings — and keep doing it.
