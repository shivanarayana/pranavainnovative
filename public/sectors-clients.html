Business sector experience

Our experience spans many business sectors, and we have had the good fortune to work with many diverse organisations — large and small.

Where we work

Broad experience, without the blinkers

We don’t limit ourselves to a single industry, but our business sector experience is perhaps greatest in the following areas. Get in touch for a use case relevant to your sector.

Retail

Customer records and transactions, product management across multiple lines and pricing, and a better customer experience.

Pharma / Healthcare

Compliance management, case oversight and clear process visibility in highly regulated environments.

Public Services & Government

Automated permit applications, inspections and case management that keep services moving.

Manufacturing & Consumer Electronics

Automated access to customer information and product availability for engineers and reps in the field.

Technology

Application integration, process optimisation and hyperautomation for fast-moving technology businesses.

Finance & Banking

Loans and mortgages, account opening and closing, fraud notifications and core-banking integration.

Process-related challenges

Complex and commonplace

The process-related challenges facing these sectors — and many others — are both complex and commonplace.

As a consultancy-led firm, our role is to work with clients to acknowledge and understand these challenges and many more, agree the key objectives, and determine the best solution to achieve those goals.
